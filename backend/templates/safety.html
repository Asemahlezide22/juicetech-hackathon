{{ bolt() }} Your phone, too

Our cables cannot carry data.

A USB connector has four pins: two for power, two for data. In every Juice Tech cable the two data pins are physically absent — not switched off, not blocked in software, simply not there. Nothing can move between the cabinet and your phone.

Every cable is tethered into the bank, so nobody can swap it for a tampered one.
